## ja

時間と範囲を指す注釈（target が #t=…&xywh=… など）を、その時間のあいだだけ動画の上に枠で出します。注釈タブの一覧には時刻が付き、押すとその時刻へ移ります。AI で見つけた人物の枠を IIIF の注釈にした、信州デジタルコモンズの映像で試せます。

## en

Annotations that point at a time and a region of a video (a target like #t=…&xywh=…) are drawn as boxes over the video while their time plays. The list in the Annotations tab shows each time, and clicking one goes there. Try it on a Shinshu Digital Commons film with boxes on people found by AI, written as IIIF annotations.
